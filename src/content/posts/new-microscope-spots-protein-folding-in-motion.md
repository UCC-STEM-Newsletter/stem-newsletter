A lab imaging technique could help drug researchers observe structural changes that were previously inferred.

## Why It Matters

Biology tools are turning dynamic processes into visible design inputs.

Seeing motion can reduce guesswork in early drug discovery and materials research.

## What To Watch

Expect more AI biology teams to pair models with richer experimental feedback.

## The Bottom Line

The technique is promising; whether it becomes routine depends on how easily other labs can reproduce it.
