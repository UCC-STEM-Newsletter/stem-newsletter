The field is making incremental gains in stability, but useful quantum advantage still depends on systems engineering.

## Why It Matters

Quantum progress is increasingly measured in reliability curves.

Error correction determines whether a device can run long enough to solve meaningful workloads.

## What To Watch

The best updates will explain overhead and error rates, not only qubit counts.

## The Bottom Line

Qubit counts make headlines, but error rates are what decide whether any of this becomes useful.
