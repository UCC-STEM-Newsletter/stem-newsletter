A new hardware cohort is designing around thermal limits as data centers search for denser compute.

## Why It Matters

The rack is becoming the product boundary for AI hardware teams.

Performance claims are harder to evaluate without power, cooling, and maintenance assumptions.

## What To Watch

Thermal design language is moving from engineering notes into investor decks.

## The Bottom Line

Thermal limits are an engineering constraint before they are a business one, and the physics does not negotiate.
