Autonomous vehicles are giving climate researchers a sharper view of subsurface temperature shifts.

## Why It Matters

The ocean data gap is narrowing through cheaper autonomous measurement.

Subsurface heat patterns influence storms, fisheries, shipping, and long-range climate models.

## What To Watch

Robotic fleets may become core scientific infrastructure rather than special expeditions.

## The Bottom Line

More measurements will not settle climate questions on their own, but they make the arguments harder to have in the abstract.
