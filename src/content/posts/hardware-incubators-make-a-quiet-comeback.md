Robotics, climate, and semiconductor founders are returning to shared labs as prototyping costs rise.

## Why It Matters

The shared lab is becoming a strategic advantage for capital-efficient hardware teams.

Access to testing gear, operators, and supplier knowledge can save months before a seed round.

## What To Watch

Expect more incubators to specialize by tooling stack rather than broad startup stage.
