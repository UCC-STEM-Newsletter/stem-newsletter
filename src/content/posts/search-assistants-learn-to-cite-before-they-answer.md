Publishers and enterprise buyers are pushing AI search products toward stronger source presentation.

## Why It Matters

Citation quality is becoming a product feature instead of a footnote.

Readers need to see why an answer exists, and publishers need traffic paths that are not invisible.

## What To Watch

The next interface fight will be over source prominence, not only answer quality.

## The Bottom Line

A citation is only useful if a reader can follow it to something that genuinely says what was claimed.
