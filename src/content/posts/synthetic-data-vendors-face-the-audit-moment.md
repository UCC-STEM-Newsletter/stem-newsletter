Buyers want proof that generated datasets improve model behavior without leaking sensitive patterns.

## Why It Matters

Synthetic data is moving from growth hack to compliance artifact.

The strongest vendors can explain provenance, coverage gaps, and failure modes in the same sales call.

## What To Watch

Expect evaluation reports to become part of standard AI procurement packets.

## The Bottom Line

Provenance is becoming the first question buyers ask and the hardest one to answer well.
